How do I find my typical views?

Sponsors pay for the people who see their video, so use the views of one normal video, not your yearly total.

  1. Open your stats (on YouTube: YouTube Studio → Content, the Views column).
  2. Write down the views of your last 9 videos. Skip very new ones that are still growing.
  3. Put them in order and take the middle one (the 5th). One viral hit won't fool it.
Where does a rate per 1,000 views come from?

Best: from a past deal. Fill in its price and views in step 2 and leave the rate empty here. The tool works it out.

No deals yet? If you're in the YouTube Partner Program, YouTube Studio → Analytics → Revenue shows your RPM, what YouTube pays you per 1,000 views from ads. A sponsor usually pays more than that, so treat it as a minimum.

What else can I charge for?

The range covers one video. Price these separately, or say no to them:

  • Usage rights: the brand reuses your video in its own ads.
  • Exclusivity: no competing brands for a set time.
  • Extra posts: Stories, Shorts, a pinned comment, a link in the description.
  • Extra revisions beyond the ones you agreed.
